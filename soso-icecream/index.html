How to play

Tap to pick things up and tap again to use them (dragging works too). Make what's in the bubble, then drag the finished cup to the customer (or tap it, then tap the customer) to serve it.

Ice cream cupCup on the mat → scoop (1–2) → sprinkles? → spoon
Waffle cupLadle batter into the cooker → pull the lid down → pull it up when ✓ → put the waffle in a cup → scoops → sprinkles? → spoon
SmoothieScoops into the blender → turn the knob ON → turn it OFF in short (yellow) or long (green) → pour into a drink cup → sprinkles? → cup cap → straw-spoon. Not sure which flavours a smoothie has? Check the recipe book beside the bin.
Ice cream sodaDrink cup under the dispenser → hold the flavour button until full → 1 scoop → sprinkles? → straw-spoon

Rinse the scoop in the bowl before switching flavours · burnt waffles, overflowing cups and watery smoothies go in the bin.
